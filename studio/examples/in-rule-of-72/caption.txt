Aapka paisa double kab hoga? Bas 72 ko divide karo. ⏱️

72 / return % = saal
• PPF 7.1% → ~10 saal
• Equity 12% (maan ke, guaranteed nahi) → ~6 saal
• 30 saal, 12% pe: ₹1 lakh → ~₹30 lakh

Ulta bhi chalta hai: mehngai 6% maan lo → 12 saal mein ₹100 ka saamaan ₹200 ka.

Save karo, har investment pe yeh check karna. Aapka paisa kitne saal mein double ho raha hai? 👇
