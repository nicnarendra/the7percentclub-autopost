Stop-loss lagaya, phir neeche khiska diya? Yeh loss aversion hai: chhota loss maanna itna dard deta hai ki hum use bada loss bana dete hain. Stop-loss ki sabse common galti aur ek simple rule, 50 second mein.

Is video ke numbers:
• ₹100 pe buy, ₹95 SL; SL ko ₹85 tak khiskana = ₹5 ka loss ₹20 ka (4x) — sirf samjhane ka example
• Recovery maths: −20% ke baad +25% chahiye (80 x 1.25 = 100); −50% ke baad +100% chahiye
• SEBI ki FY26 study (Aug 2026): equity F&O mein kareeb 88% individual traders ne net loss kiya; aggregate loss ~₹91,685 crore
Sources: SEBI study on individual traders in equity derivatives (FY26), Business Today, Moneylife
