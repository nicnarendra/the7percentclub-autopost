Intraday trading se roz ki kamaai? SEBI ki study kehti hai 10 mein se 7 individual intraday traders loss mein the (FY23, equity cash segment). Young traders, zyada trades aur charges ka pura sach 50 second mein.

Is video ke numbers (SEBI study on intraday trading, press release 24 July 2024, FY23 data):
• 70% se zyada individual intraday traders ko FY23 mein loss
• FY19 se FY23 ke beech intraday traders 300% se zyada badhe
• 30 saal se kam umar waalon ka share: 18% (FY19) se 48% (FY23); inmein 76% loss mein
• Saal mein 500 se zyada trades karne waalon mein 80% loss mein
• Loss waalon ne apne loss ka 57% extra trading costs mein diya; profit waalon ne profit ka 19%
• ₹10,000 loss par ~₹5,700 charges: sirf samjhane ka example
Sources: SEBI press release (24 Jul 2024), Business Today, Taxmann
