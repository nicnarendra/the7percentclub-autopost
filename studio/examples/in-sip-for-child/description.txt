Bacche ke janam se ₹10,000 monthly SIP, 18 saal baad kitna banega? 12% saal ka return maan ke (guaranteed nahi): kareeb ₹76 lakh, jabki aapka paisa sirf ₹21.6 lakh. Aur 5 saal late shuru kiya toh rakam aadhi se bhi kam.

Is video ke numbers (sab 12% saalana return maan ke, monthly compounding; asli returns upar-neeche hote hain, guaranteed nahi):
• ₹10,000/mahina x 216 mahine = ₹21.6 lakh invest
• 18 saal baad: ~₹76.5 lakh (growth ~₹55 lakh)
• Pehle 5 saal: ₹6 lakh daale, value ~₹8.25 lakh
• Saal 15 se 18: ~₹50.5 lakh se ~₹76.5 lakh, yaani ~₹26 lakh sirf aakhri 3 saal mein
• Age 5 se shuru (13 saal): ~₹37.6 lakh
Formula: FV = P × [((1+r)^n − 1)/r] × (1+r), r = 1%/mahina (standard SIP calculator formula)
