Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The 7% Club ("we") runs a small private tool, "7club autopost", that uploads our own videos to our own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account.

What the tool accesses

The tool signs in only to accounts owned by The 7% Club. It uses Google's YouTube Data API with the youtube.upload permission to upload videos to our channel, and Meta's Graph API to publish Reels to our Facebook Page and Instagram account.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data about viewers or anyone else. The tool does not read viewers' data. It stores only the IDs and links of the videos it posts.

How access is kept safe

Access keys are stored as encrypted secrets and are used only to post our own videos. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account permissions page or Meta's Business Integrations settings.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This service uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
