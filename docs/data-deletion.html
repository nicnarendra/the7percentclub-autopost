User data deletion

7club autopost is a private tool used only by the owner of The 7% Club to publish its own videos to its own Facebook Page and Instagram account. It does not let other people log in, and it does not collect, store or share personal data about Facebook or Instagram users.

How to remove access or request deletion

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find 7club autopost and click Remove. This revokes the app's access immediately.
  2. If you believe the app holds any data about you, email nps170293@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.
